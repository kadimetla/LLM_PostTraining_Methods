02 Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) 📓 notebook

03 Reward Modeling 📓 notebook

Before you can optimize with RL, you need a signal: the reward model (RM). The RM is trained on human preference pairs — "response A is better than B" — and learns to score any output. We build one from a small SFT checkpoint, exploring the Bradley-Terry model and preference loss.

04 RLHF with PPO 📓 notebook

Proximal Policy Optimization closes the loop: generate samples from the policy, score them with the reward model, and update the policy to maximize that score while staying close to the SFT checkpoint (KL penalty). This is the original InstructGPT approach. We run a small PPO experiment using TRL's PPOTrainer.

05 Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) 📓 notebook

DPO eliminates the reward model and RL loop entirely. It reparameterizes the RLHF objective so that the policy itself is the reward model — you optimize on preference pairs with a simple cross-entropy style loss. Cleaner, cheaper, and surprisingly effective. We compare DPO checkpoints against the PPO run.

Advanced & Cutting Edge
06 GRPO & Reasoning Models 📓 notebook

Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) was the secret behind DeepSeek-R1's reasoning ability — no value function, just a group of sampled outputs scored against verifiable rewards (math answers, code correctness). We train a tiny model to reason step-by-step using rule-based rewards, matching the DeepSeek approach at small scale.
